How to read it
· Coin Flip is a control: same entry timestamps, random direction. A strategy that cannot beat it has no directional edge.
· Per-trade return matters more than total return — totals are driven by trade count.
· Rankings before a few hundred trades are mostly luck; per-trade standard deviation is tens of times the mean.
